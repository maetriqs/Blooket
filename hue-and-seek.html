How to play

Hiders get time to paint before anyone looks. Open your paint kit with P, grab colors off the floor with the picker and paint your body until it matches what's under you. Then stay still. If you move, your paint moves with you.

Seekers keep their eyes shut while the hiders paint, then click anything that looks off. Every miss leaves you dizzy for a moment.

Hiders chirp now and then, which gives away where they are. Press T to chirp on purpose for bonus points.
