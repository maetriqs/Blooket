How to play

Hiders get time to paint before anyone looks. Find a spot, press P and paint straight onto your body. Right-click anything in the room to pick up its color. Then stay still. If you move, your paint moves with you.

Seekers keep their eyes shut while the hiders paint, then click anything that looks off. Every miss leaves you dizzy for a moment.

Move with WASD, drag to look around, Space jumps. Hiders turn with Q and E. Hiders chirp now and then, which gives away where they are. T chirps on purpose for bonus points.
